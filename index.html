How is a date idea different from a profile?

A date idea gives you something specific to respond to, such as coffee and a walk or a visit to a bookstore. You can still explore the person’s photos, preferences, and Relationship Profile before deciding.

Can I share my ideal first date?

Yes. Create a date card with the first date you’d love to go on. Other people can respond to your idea, and you choose whose reply to accept.

When can we start chatting?

A private chat opens when a reply is accepted. The 24-hour chat window gives you time to exchange messages and agree on your date.

Is DateNow available on the App Store?

DateNow is currently being tested on iPhone. We’ll share the public App Store link here when it’s available. DateNow is for adults 18 and older.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the menu on a person’s date card to report or block them. For help with a safety concern, contact info@datenow.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, select Edit profile, and scroll to the Account section. Choose Delete account and confirm. See account support for more information.
