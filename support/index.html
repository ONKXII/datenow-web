Contact support

Tell us what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS and DateNow app versions. For sign-in help, include the email address associated with your account.

Keep passwords, verification codes, payment card details, and identity documents out of your message.

Manage or delete your account

To permanently delete your account in DateNow, follow these steps.

  1. Open Profile and select Edit profile.
  2. Scroll to the Account section.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm the deletion.

Deletion removes your profile and associated content. Our Privacy Policy explains retention required for legal and security purposes.

If you cannot access your account, email info@datenow.com to request help. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you.

Deleting your DateNow account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage any active subscription separately through your Apple account.

Report a concern

Use the menu on a person’s date card to report or block them. You can also email info@datenow.com with the relevant details.

DateNow is for adults 18 and older. Choose a public place for a first meeting and let someone you trust know your plans.

Subscriptions and purchases

For an Apple subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select DateNow to view or manage an active subscription. See Apple’s subscription instructions for more help.

For an Apple purchase refund request, visit Apple’s purchase support. For help with access to a purchased DateNow feature, contact info@datenow.com.

Privacy and legal information

Read our User Agreement and Privacy Policy. For a privacy request, contact info@datenow.com.
